## Gender Equality Goals

**At least 30% of female researchers recruited and implement measures to support gender equality, diversity, family-friendliness, and work-life balance**.

### Measures

> **Encouraging women to apply for positions**

> **Monitoring of gender balance**

> **Organization of a diversity and gender equality workshop**

> **Improving work-life balance through flexible hours, part-time work, and remote working opportunities**

## Gender Equality Board

A Gender and Equality Board (GEB) is being established, in which all status groups are represented (mixed female/male) and all institutions are involved. The objectives of the GEB are to:

1. Propose, explore, and decide on equal-opportunity measures for DFG SOS
2. Coordinate the implementation of equal-opportunity measures
3. Decide on the use of the equal-opportunity funds available within DFG SOS and align their use with DFG guidelines (with support from the Equality Office of JMU Würzburg).
